47 CFR 80.401 Station documents requirement. Transcription of the table (the eCFR shows it as an image). R = REQUIRED. Numbers in parentheses are the table's footnote numbers; footnotes 1-5 are printed below the table in the rule, and footnote 6 appears in the table but is not printed.
Columns (18): Station License | Appropriate Operator Authorization | Station Logs | Appropriate Safety Convention Certificate | Communications Act Safety Certificate | Great Lakes Safety Certificate | Bridge-to-Bridge Act Safety Certificate | Part 80; FCC Rules and Regulations | Alphabetical List of Maritime Mobile Call Signs | List of Ship Stations | Manual for Use by Maritime Mobile (M/M) Service and M/M Satellite Service | List of Coast Stations | List of Radiodetermination and Special Services Stations | Station Equipment Records | GMDSS Master Plan | NGA Publication 117 | Admiralty List of Radio Signals | IMO Circ. 7. Cargo ships (300 tons and up): Station License R(1); Appropriate Operator Authorization R; Station Logs R; Appropriate Safety Convention Certificate R; Part 80; FCC Rules and Regulations R; Alphabetical List of Maritime Mobile Call Signs R(6); List of Ship Stations R(6); Manual for Use by Maritime Mobile (M/M) Service and M/M Satellite Service R; List of Coast Stations R(5); List of Radiodetermination and Special Services Stations R; GMDSS Master Plan R(5); NGA Publication 117 R; Admiralty List of Radio Signals R(5); IMO Circ. 7 R(5).
Passenger Vessels – SOLAS: Station License R(1); Appropriate Operator Authorization R; Station Logs R; Communications Act Safety Certificate R; Part 80; FCC Rules and Regulations R; Alphabetical List of Maritime Mobile Call Signs R; List of Ship Stations R; Manual for Use by Maritime Mobile (M/M) Service and M/M Satellite Service R; List of Coast Stations R(5); List of Radiodetermination and Special Services Stations R; GMDSS Master Plan R(5); NGA Publication 117 R; Admiralty List of Radio Signals R(5); IMO Circ. 7 R(5).
Note 5 (printed with the rule): The requirements for having the GMDSS Master Plan, NGA Publication 117, Admiralty List of Radio Signals or IMO Circ. 7 are satisfied by having any one of those four documents.
